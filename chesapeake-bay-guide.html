Chesapeake Bay Boating Guide

Historic ports, quiet creeks, legendary seafood and enough water for a lifetime of cruising.

The Chesapeake's scale creates nearly unlimited itineraries, from polished Annapolis weekends to quiet anchorages and working-waterfront towns. It rewards slower travel and careful selection of a small region for each trip.

Best seasonMay–June and September–October
Good baseAnnapolis
Trip styleMulti-day cruising

Start smaller than the map suggests

For a first visit, choose Annapolis and one additional port instead of chasing mileage. St. Michaels, Oxford and nearby creeks can each anchor a complete weekend depending on conditions and available dockage.

Respect shallow water and summer weather

The bay contains extensive shoal water, busy channels and rapidly building thunderstorms. Current charts, weather awareness and conservative routing matter more than ambitious schedules.

Before you depart

Confirm marina or anchorage plans, bridge and depth limitations, fuel, marine alerts and severe-weather options. Heat and humidity also make hydration and shade essential for family crews.

Captain's note: Rules, operating schedules, depths, weather and dock availability change. Confirm current information with the applicable state, harbor, marina and navigation authorities before departure.
