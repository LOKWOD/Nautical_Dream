Erie Canal Boating Guide

Lock-through adventure, canal towns and a slower kind of boating across the heart of New York.

The Erie Canal turns the voyage itself into the destination. Locks, lift bridges and historic towns create a slower rhythm that is especially appealing to crews who enjoy planning, local stops and conversation along the route.

Best seasonJune through September
Good baseSegment-based
Trip styleSlow travel & locks

Choose a section for your first trip

Rather than attempting the entire canal, select a manageable segment with dependable overnight stops. Central New York offers easy access to canal communities, while western sections connect naturally with the Finger Lakes and Niagara region.

Learn lock procedure before arrival

Prepare lines and fenders on both sides, follow lockmaster instructions and keep hands clear of walls and hardware. Locking is straightforward when the crew is briefed before entering.

Before you cast off

Check the New York State Canal Corporation’s current operating schedule, notices, closures, lock dimensions and bridge clearances. Water levels and maintenance can change the route.

Captain's note: Rules, operating schedules, depths, weather and dock availability change. Confirm current information with the applicable state, harbor, marina and navigation authorities before departure.
