Life Jackets People Will Actually Wear

Fit, rating and comfort guidance for adults, children and different kinds of boating.

The right life jacket is properly approved, correctly sized and comfortable enough to remain on—not stored under a seat.

Fit comes first

Follow the manufacturer’s weight and chest-size ranges. A correctly fitted jacket should remain secure when lifted at the shoulders and should not ride over the wearer’s face.

Match the jacket to the activity

Cruising, paddling, watersports and offshore use place different demands on flotation, mobility and visibility. Confirm the approval label and intended use before purchase.

Children require direct supervision

Use age- and weight-appropriate flotation with the required head support and leg straps. A life jacket supports safety; it never replaces attentive adult supervision.
