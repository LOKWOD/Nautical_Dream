Cape Cod Boating Guide

Harbors, beaches and salt-air towns connected by very different waters on the bay and Atlantic sides.

Cape Cod is not one single boating experience. Protected harbors, Cape Cod Bay and the Atlantic side each demand different planning, creating exceptional variety for crews prepared for coastal conditions.

Best seasonJune through September
Good baseHyannis, Falmouth or Provincetown
Trip styleCoastal harbor hopping

Pick the right harbor, not just the right town

Hyannis offers central access and services, Falmouth suits southwest routes, and Provincetown creates a dramatic destination at the tip. Base the choice on wind, travel distance and the boat—not only the restaurant list.

Keep the itinerary weather-flexible

Build a primary route and a protected-water alternative. Tides, fog, wind and exposed stretches can make a planned crossing inappropriate even when the harbor looks calm.

Before you launch

Check tides, marine forecasts, local harbor rules, reservations, fuel and navigation notices. Never treat a straight line on a map as a guaranteed route through shallow coastal water.

Captain's note: Rules, operating schedules, depths, weather and dock availability change. Confirm current information with the applicable state, harbor, marina and navigation authorities before departure.
