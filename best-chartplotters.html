How Much Chartplotter Do You Really Need?

Choose the right display, mapping and sonar features without paying for equipment you will never use.

The best chartplotter is the one you can read in direct sun, operate in rough water and understand without digging through menus.

Start with how you boat

Inland cruising prioritizes clear mapping and an easy interface. Fishing adds sonar and transducer compatibility. Coastal use raises the value of radar, AIS and network integration.

Screen size beats feature count

Buy the largest display that fits the helm without blocking sightlines or controls. A crowded small screen can make excellent cartography difficult to use.

Before choosing a brand

Confirm chart coverage, transducer requirements, power draw, mounting depth, software-update support and compatibility with equipment already aboard.
