What matters aboard

Measure the footprint and lid swing. Confirm that the drain remains accessible, feet will not mark the deck and a strap can hold the cooler in place. Then estimate loaded weight—not just the empty specification.

Our rule

Choose the smallest cooler that comfortably handles your normal outing. Buy for the trips you actually take, not the imaginary week offshore.
