Lake Champlain Boating Guide

Island passages, mountain horizons and historic waterfront towns on one enormous cruising lake.

Lake Champlain feels more like an inland sea than a conventional lake. Long sightlines, changing wind, broad crossings and island routes reward boaters who plan deliberately while still leaving room to explore.

Best seasonJune through September
Good baseBurlington or Plattsburgh
Trip styleInland cruising

Build the trip around a home port

Burlington offers a lively waterfront and an easy walk into town. Plattsburgh provides practical access from the New York side, while the Champlain Islands create a quieter base for coves, beaches and shorter day routes.

A sensible two-day route

Use the first day for the Burlington waterfront and a protected shoreline cruise. Reserve the second for the islands or a longer north-south run only after checking wind and wave conditions.

Before you launch

Confirm launch hours, transient slips, border rules if entering Canadian waters, fuel stops and the marine forecast. Distances are deceptive here, and afternoon wind can transform an easy crossing.

Captain's note: Rules, operating schedules, depths, weather and dock availability change. Confirm current information with the applicable state, harbor, marina and navigation authorities before departure.
