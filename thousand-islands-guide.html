A Thousand Islands Boating Weekend

Castles, channels, coves and the planning details that make this border-water trip work.

The Thousand Islands are best approached as a cruising region, not a checklist. Pick a home base, understand the border rules and leave room for slow exploration.

Choose a practical home base

Clayton and Alexandria Bay provide convenient access to fuel, food, marinas and the central islands. Reserve dockage before busy summer weekends.

Plan one landmark and one quiet stretch

Pair a major stop such as Boldt Castle with time in quieter channels. Trying to cover the entire region in two days turns a memorable cruise into a fuel run.

Navigation and border awareness

Carry current charts, monitor weather and understand customs requirements before crossing into Canadian waters or landing. Confirm all rules with the appropriate authorities before departure.
