Buying guide

Boat coolers that earn their deck space.

A practical guide to choosing a durable cooler that fits your boat and the way you use it.

The best boat cooler is the one that fits your boat

Capacity, footprint, weight, drainage and hold time matter more than hype. A family pontoon, fishing skiff and overnight cruiser need different solutions.

Best overall approach

Choose the smallest premium rotomolded cooler that comfortably holds a full day of drinks and food. Oversized coolers waste deck space, become difficult to move and invite overpacking.

What to look for

  • Secure latching and non-slip feet
  • Drain placement that works on your deck
  • Enough clearance for lids and seating
  • Comfortable handles for a fully loaded cooler
  • Replacement parts from an established manufacturer
