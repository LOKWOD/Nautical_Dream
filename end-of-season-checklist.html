Maintenance

A Better End-of-Season Checklist

A sensible maintenance sequence for batteries, fuel, plumbing, upholstery and spring readiness.

Good winterization is a documented sequence, not a last-minute collection of chores.

Stabilize and protect systems

Follow the engine, fuel-system, plumbing and battery manufacturers’ procedures. Freeze protection and corrosion prevention vary by equipment and climate.

Clean before covering

Dry compartments, remove food and soft goods where appropriate, clean drains and address mildew before storage. Ventilation matters as much as the cover.

Create the spring list now

Record repairs, part numbers, fluid changes and anything deferred. Store the checklist with the boat records so launch preparation starts with facts.
