What Makes a Great Family Boat?

Layout, shade, storage and boarding details that matter more than maximum horsepower.

Family boats succeed through hundreds of small interactions: getting aboard, finding gear, moving safely and staying comfortable for hours.

Start with movement

Walk the boat as if everyone is carrying something. Wide paths, secure handholds and predictable step heights matter when children and guests are aboard.

Shade and storage change the day

Usable shade extends time on the water. Dedicated storage for flotation, lines, food and wet gear keeps the deck safer and calmer.

Buy for normal use

Choose around the people, water and activities that make up most outings. Occasional ambitions should not dominate every ownership decision.
